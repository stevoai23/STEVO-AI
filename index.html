⚡ EVERYTHING THAT USES CURRENT

🔍 SEARCH BAR - Search Anything That Uses Current
What Is Current?
Current is electricity flowing, like water in a pipe. Voltage = pressure. Resistance = blockage. Formula: V = I x R
Phone & Charger
Charger changes AC from NEPA to DC for phone battery. If phone is not charging: change cable, change charger, clean port, restart phone.
Car
Battery gives current to starter motor. Alternator charges battery while engine runs.
Solar
Panel takes light from sun and makes DC current. Inverter changes DC to AC for house.
Fridge / Fan / TV
All use motor or screen that needs current to work. No current = no work.
