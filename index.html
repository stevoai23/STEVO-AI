What Is Current?
Current is electricity flowing, like water flowing in a pipe. Voltage is pressure. Resistance is blockage. Formula: V = I x R
Phone & Charger
Charger changes AC from NEPA to DC for your phone battery. If phone is not charging, check cable, charger, socket, charging port.
Car
Car battery gives current to starter motor to start engine. Alternator charges battery while engine runs.
Solar
Solar panel takes light from sun and makes DC current. Inverter changes DC to AC for house.
